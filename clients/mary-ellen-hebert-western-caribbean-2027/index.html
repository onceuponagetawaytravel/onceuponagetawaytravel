What's a Virtual Balcony?

An interior stateroom with a floor-to-ceiling high-definition screen showing a live outdoor view. It is a screen, not a physical balcony. February 21 starts at $848.94 per person. Important balcony difference

Neighborhood View Balcony: faces an interior ship neighborhood such as the Boardwalk, not the ocean. Ocean View Balcony: faces outward toward the sea. For February 21, the ocean-facing balcony starts at $1,357.94; February 28 starts around $1,451.94. Confirm the specific cabin's view before selecting.

SHIP SPOTLIGHT

Fun facts about Symphony of the Seas

There's so much to enjoy between port days! From ice skating to robot bartenders, this ship has plenty of surprises.

⛸️ Ice skating at sea

Yes, an actual ice rink! Watch professional ice shows and look for guest skating sessions in the daily schedule.

🎭 Broadway and spectacular shows

Enjoy productions including Hairspray, thrilling AquaTheater diving performances, and the ship's ice spectaculars. Exact showtimes and lineup may change.

🎤 Game night and karaoke

Enjoy onboard games and karaoke at On Air Club. Interested in Family Feud? Ask us to check the entertainment schedule, since a Family Feud event is not confirmed for these sailings.

🍹 Bars with personality

Meet robot bartenders at Bionic Bar, listen to piano music at Schooner Bar, enjoy Latin rhythms at Boleros, or ride the moving Rising Tide Bar between decks.

🍽️ Food for every mood

Try included options such as the Main Dining Room, Windjammer, Sorrento's Pizza and Park Café. Specialty choices include Chops Grille, Wonderland, Hooked Seafood and Izumi, generally for an additional charge.

🌊 Big-time thrills

Take on the Ultimate Abyss slide, Perfect Storm waterslides, FlowRider surf simulator, rock climbing walls and glow-in-the-dark laser tag. Some experiences have restrictions or limited sessions.

Entertainment, dining venues and activity schedules can change. Some dining, beverages and experiences cost extra. Check the Royal Caribbean app closer to sailing.
