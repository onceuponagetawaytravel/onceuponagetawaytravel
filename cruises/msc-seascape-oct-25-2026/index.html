🛂 Travel Documents • Don't Leave Home Without Them!

For U.S. citizens sailing round-trip from Galveston: bring one of the following approved document options for check-in:

✅ Option 1: Valid U.S. Passport

A passport book is our preferred choice. MSC recommends that it remain valid for at least six months after your cruise ends. A passport book also makes it possible to fly internationally if an emergency occurs.

✅ Option 2: Certified Birth Certificate + Photo ID

Bring your original or state-certified copy of your U.S. birth certificate AND a current, unexpired government-issued photo ID, such as your driver's license, if age 16 or older. Hospital keepsake certificates and ordinary photocopies do not qualify.

💍 Different last names? If your birth certificate, driver's license, passport, or cruise reservation show different names, bring the original or certified marriage certificate/license or other legal name-change document connecting them. Contact your travel advisor before sailing to confirm your reservation matches your documents.

👧 Traveling with children? U.S. citizen children under 16 can generally use a government-issued birth certificate or other accepted citizenship document. Additional documentation may be required when surnames differ or when traveling without a parent or legal guardian.

Important: These options apply to eligible U.S. citizens on this round-trip itinerary. Non-U.S. citizens and other situations may require a passport, visa or additional documents. Keep all original travel documents in your carry-on, not checked luggage. Missing required documents may result in denied boarding.

👗 What to Wear Onboard

Tap any topic to see MSC's attire recommendations and restrictions.

☀️ Daytime Attire

Casual clothing is recommended. Shorts and T-shirts are permitted.

🌙 Evening Wear

For main dining rooms, specialty restaurants and evening entertainment, MSC recommends a more polished look. Collared shirts, tailored trousers, skirts and dresses are suggested. Bring a light jacket or sweater for cool evenings and air-conditioned spaces.

✨ Gala Night

Dress to your own level of elegance, from tuxedos and evening gowns to your best jeans and polo shirt. Gala Night is an opportunity to dress up, but formalwear is not mandatory. Note the dress restrictions below.

🎉 Theme Parties

MSC organizes daytime and evening themed parties. Bright, fun clothing works for Sunshine Party, and white outfits for White Party. For this sailing, consider tropical prints, retro looks and a family-friendly Halloween costume. Activities and themes are subject to the onboard schedule.

⚓ MSC Yacht Club & Sports

Yacht Club guests should observe venue-specific dress rules. For the gym, yoga and dance classes, pack gym shoes and activewear.

🏝️ Shore Excursions & Local Customs

For religious sites, cover knees and shoulders where required. Follow local laws and cultural expectations at every port. Some destinations prohibit camouflage or clothing resembling military or police uniforms, with possible penalties. Research your destinations before packing.

🚫 Dress Restrictions

No swimwear in formal dining venues at any meal. Shorts, flip-flops and tank tops are not allowed for evening wear or Gala Night in main dining rooms, specialty restaurants or MSC Yacht Club. T-shirts are not allowed on Gala Night. Follow posted buffet dress codes and onboard staff guidance.

For complete, current rules, visit MSC Cruises Official FAQs ↗. Onboard staff may enforce additional restrictions.
