What your cruise generally includes

Your stateroom, most main dining and buffet meals, onboard entertainment and access to many ship amenities. Specific inclusions depend on the cruise line, fare package and cabin category. Specialty restaurants, alcoholic beverages, Wi-Fi, spa services, excursions, travel insurance, parking, transportation and certain onboard activities may cost extra.

Important details before booking

Booking age: This honeymoon quote is intended for guests age 21 and older. Cruise-line minimum ages, required accompanying adults and individual occupancy policies must be confirmed before booking.

Taxes and fees: Carnival Liberty's quoted rates already include $158 in taxes and fees and $159 in port charges per the supplier quote. These amounts are not added again. Tax and port-fee inclusion for the other three sailings has not been confirmed.

Gratuities: The estimates above use MSC standard $17 per guest per night, Royal Caribbean standard $18.50 (suite $21), and Carnival standard $17 (suite $19). Confirm whether gratuities are already included or prepaid before adding them to any final booking total. Rates can change.

Prices and availability: Quote prepared November 16, 2026. Starting fares are not held, and cabins, promotions, taxes and availability may change until booked. Deposit, final payment, cancellation and refund terms will be provided with the verified booking quote. Port schedules and excursions are subject to change.

Suggested honeymoon extras: Consider a balcony, couples spa treatment, specialty dinner, shore excursion or travel protection. These are optional and are not included in the quoted fares.
