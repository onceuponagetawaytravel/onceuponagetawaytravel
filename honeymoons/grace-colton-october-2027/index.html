What your cruise generally includes

Your stateroom, most main dining and buffet meals, onboard entertainment and access to many ship amenities. Specific inclusions depend on the cruise line, fare package and cabin category. Specialty restaurants, alcoholic beverages, Wi-Fi, spa services, excursions, travel insurance, parking, transportation and certain onboard activities may cost extra.

Important Details Before Booking

Age Requirements: Cruise lines have minimum age requirements for booking a stateroom. Generally, at least one guest must be 21 or older, although policies vary by cruise line and sailing.

Taxes & Port Fees: All pricing shown includes applicable taxes and port fees. These amounts vary depending on the cruise line, ship, and itinerary.

Gratuities: Gratuities are NOT included in the advertised pricing. They are an additional expense that can typically be prepaid before sailing or charged to your onboard account.

Estimated standard gratuities per guest, per night:

  • MSC Cruises: $17
  • Royal Caribbean: $18.50 (Suites: $21)

Gratuity rates are subject to change and will be confirmed before booking.
