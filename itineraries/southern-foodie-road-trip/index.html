A Southern Foodie Road Trip

Port Canaveral to Beaumont

October 24-27, 2026. Three nights, charming small towns, foodie stops, coastal scenery, Cajun Country, and an easy final drive home to Beaumont by midday Tuesday.

3 nightsDeFuniak Springs, Fairhope, Breaux Bridge
Foodie focusedOld Florida lunch, scratch Italian, Gulf seafood, Cajun classics
Scenic stopsMicanopy, Lake DeFuniak, Mobile Bay, Avery Island
Home by middayEasy final morning from Cajun Country
Your road trip home should still feel like vacation. This itinerary keeps you moving west while giving each day its own personality: Old Florida shopping and lunch, a historic lake town, Mobile Bay at sunset, then authentic Cajun Country before an easy final drive home.
