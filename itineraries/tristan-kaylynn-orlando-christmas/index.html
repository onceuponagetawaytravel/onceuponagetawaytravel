The Itinerary

THU
DEC 10

Arrive in Orlando ✈️

Fly into Orlando, transfer to Universal's Endless Summer Resort – Dockside Inn and Suites, check in and settle in. Keep tonight easy. Explore the resort or head to Universal CityWalk for dinner.

FRI
DEC 11

Universal Studios Florida + Islands of Adventure

Use the Park-to-Park ticket and make this your Wizarding World day. Explore Diagon Alley, ride Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts™, then take the Hogwarts Express™ to Hogsmeade. If choosing the Exclusive Package, work the included themed breakfast experiences into these two park days.

SAT
DEC 12

Universal + Islands, Round Two

Use the second Park-to-Park day for everything you missed, favorite repeats and the second Wizarding World breakfast. This is also the day to enjoy the Shutterbutton's session and Florean Fortescue's Ice-Cream Flight if included in your package.

SUN
DEC 13

Universal Epic Universe 🌌

A full day dedicated to Epic Universe. Explore Celestial Park, Super Nintendo World, Dark Universe, How to Train Your Dragon – Isle of Berk, and The Wizarding World of Harry Potter – Ministry of Magic.

MON
DEC 14

Move to Disney + Hollywood Studios

Check out of Universal and transfer to your Disney resort. Start the four-day Park Hopper ticket today. Hollywood Studios is the natural park choice because Disney Jollywood Nights is offered tonight only during their stay.

Optional: Disney Jollywood Nights from 7:30 PM–12:30 AM. Event guests can enter beginning at 5:30 PM.

TUE
DEC 15

Magic Kingdom + Optional Christmas Party

Spend the day at Magic Kingdom, or take a slower morning after Jollywood. Tonight is an available Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party date, making this the ultimate Christmas-at-Disney day if they want to add it.

WED
DEC 16

EPCOT 🌐

Explore EPCOT and the holiday atmosphere around World Showcase. The Park Hopper option leaves the evening open to revisit another park if they want.

THU
DEC 17

Animal Kingdom + Hopper Finale

Start at Animal Kingdom, then use the final Park Hopper day to finish the trip wherever they want most. Return to EPCOT, Magic Kingdom or Hollywood Studios for one last Disney night.

FRI
DEC 18

Travel Home ✈️

Check out of the Disney resort and head to the airport. No park ticket is planned for today.
