Jonah's Day-by-Day Itinerary

Sunday, Oct 18 · Paw Patrol Road Trip

Depart Beaumont and use Jonah's car-spotting checklist to look for trucks, red cars, bridges and emergency vehicles. Plan snacks and breaks. A 2 PM departure may mean a late arrival or an extra overnight stop.

Monday, Oct 19 · Silver Dollar City

Make this the main park day! Explore height-appropriate rides, family entertainment, fall decorations and the Harvest Festival. Stay for glowing pumpkins if the park's event schedule allows.

Park information

Tuesday, Oct 20 · Mountain Coaster & Fall Views

Enjoy the Runaway Mountain Coaster and a scenic Ozark drive. Jonah may ride as a passenger at age 4 if he is at least 36 inches tall and accompanied by an eligible adult driver. Confirm current rules and weather before purchasing.

Coaster tickets

Wednesday, Oct 21 · Construction Fun & Exploration

Consider DIG TOWN for Jonah's love of construction vehicles. Confirm its location, operating dates and minimum ages first; do not assume it is a Branson attraction. Alternative: Fritz's Adventure, Butterfly Palace or Branson's Ferris Wheel.
