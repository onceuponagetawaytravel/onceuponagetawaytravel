Party Nights & What to Pack

MSC Seascape: recent 7-night Galveston guests have reported White Night, Gala/Dress Your Best, Tropical, Retro and Western/Texas-style nights. MSC officially promotes its Big Texas Sailaway Party and Texas-flavored entertainment from Galveston. Exact themes can change, so we will confirm Brenna’s actual lineup in the MSC for Me app closer to departure.

Liberty of the Seas: Royal Caribbean’s nightly entertainment and dress suggestions vary by sailing. Expect casual evenings plus a dressier night, live music, shows and Royal Promenade entertainment. Wait for the Royal Caribbean app’s Daily Planner before buying theme-night outfits.

First Cruise? Here's What I Want You to Know

🪪 Bring the right documentsDon't wait until cruise week to check your travel documents. I'll help make sure you know exactly what your sailing requires.
📱 Download the cruise appOnce booked, the cruise line app becomes your best friend for check-in, dining, activities and your daily schedule.
🧳 Pack a carry-on for Day 1Your checked luggage may take a while to reach the room. Keep ID, meds, swimsuit, sunscreen and anything important with you.
⏰ Ship time mattersIn port, always know your all-aboard time and be back early. The ship will not wait for late independent travelers.
🍹 Don't buy a package blindlyWe can compare the drink package cost to what you'll realistically drink before adding it.
