First Cruise? Budget for These Extras

🚗 Galveston Parking

Plan approximately $150–$250 for the week.

💵 MSC Gratuities

Gratuities are not included. Budget $18 per person, per day, approximately $252 total for two. These are added to your onboard account and settled at the end of the cruise.
