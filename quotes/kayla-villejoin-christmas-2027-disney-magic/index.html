Disney Is Not Just for Kids

Disney Magic has an adults-only side for date nights, quiet pool time, cocktails, spa time and nightlife.

🍷 Palo

Adults-only Northern Italian dining for an elevated brunch or date night. Reservations and an additional fee apply.

🌊 Quiet Cove Pool

An adults-only pool area for guests 18+, with loungers, whirlpool spas, Signals bar and nearby Cove Café.

☕ Cove Café

An adults-only lounge for specialty coffee and tea by day, then cocktails, wine and light bites later.

🎹 After Hours

Adult nightlife spaces include O'Gills Pub, Keys piano bar and Fathoms for music, dancing and entertainment.

💆 Senses Spa & Salon

Book massages, facials, salon services or other spa treatments for a quieter escape.

🥂 Tastings & Workshops

Look for adult-exclusive beverage tastings and cocktail workshops in the onboard schedule.

Take a Look Around Disney Magic 🎥

Disney Magic ship tour: See the restaurants, bars, adult pool, Palo, Cove Café, nightlife venues, outdoor decks and staterooms.
