The numbers & the fine print

What the final quote needs to include.

The supplied search links contain price values, but those values do not establish a bookable nightly rate or an all-in total. They have not been multiplied into an estimated quote.

For every qualifying stay

  • Availability for all six nights and two adults.
  • Total in USD, with the nightly breakdown.
  • Cleaning, service, lodging taxes and all mandatory fees.
  • Any refundable deposit or damage-protection charge.
  • Amount due now and exact remaining-payment deadline.
  • Cancellation cutoff, time zone and refund amount.

  • Firewood, charcoal or supplies beyond the host’s starter provisions.

A comfortable stay starts with the right questions

Before you say “that’s the one.”

Treehouse & access check

Ask for the number of steps from parking to the door, from the bed to the bathroom, and from the living area to the hot tub. Confirm ladders, lofts, steep ramps, outdoor transitions and any posted occupancy or load limits. A ramp at the entrance does not establish full accessibility.

For a true treehouse requirement, request the host’s description of how the structure is supported and current exterior photos. A property name alone is not enough.

Winter & spa check

Confirm the tub is private to your rental, heated and operating during December 28–January 3. Ask about maintenance, access hours, weather-related shutdowns and what happens if it is unavailable.

Ask the host about snow/ice procedures, driveway grade, recommended vehicle and firewood availability. Make holiday plans around current conditions, not summer listing photos.
