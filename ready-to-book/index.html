2

Your Travel Bestie Confirms the Price

Once your traveler information is submitted, your Travel Bestie will confirm the current price and availability before any payment is authorized.

Why this matters: Travel pricing and availability can change. We will make sure you know the confirmed price before moving forward with payment.
3

Complete the Credit Card Authorization

After your price is confirmed, complete the credit card authorization form for the payment you want us to process.

Planning to make payments? You will need to complete a new credit card authorization form each time you want a payment processed.

Want to pay in full? No problem! Please specify on the authorization form that you would like to pay the reservation in full.
For your security, payment information is submitted through the secure authorization form linked below rather than sent by text, email, or message.
Complete Credit Card Authorization →
What Happens Next? 🎉
Once your traveler forms are submitted, your Travel Bestie will confirm the current price and availability with you. After you approve the confirmed price, submit your credit card authorization for the payment you would like processed. Once your booking is secured, we’ll send you the reservation details and help you with the next steps for your getaway.
